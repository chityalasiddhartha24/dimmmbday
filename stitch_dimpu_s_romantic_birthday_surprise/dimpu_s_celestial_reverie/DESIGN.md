## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an intimate, cinematic, and deeply emotional digital sanctuary celebrating Dimpu's birthday. The aesthetic synthesizes high-end editorial luxury with ethereal dreaminess, avoiding childish whimsy in favor of poetic romance, warmth, and timeless celebration. 

The experience evokes feelings of awe, deep adoration, nostalgic sentimentality, and bespoke luxury. It draws from **Ethereal Glassmorphism** layered over soft ambient light, incorporating fine-jewelry delicacy, champagne-glow translucency, and gentle tactile surfaces. Visual cues reflect vintage love letters translated into a contemporary high-fashion digital editorial—featuring luminous, glowing glass panels, starlight highlights, and meticulous calligraphic nuances.

## Colors
The palette balances tender warmth with dramatic, emotive contrasts. 

- **Primary (`#881337` Deep Romantic Rose):** Serves as the anchor for emotional weight, impactful headlines, and primary actions, providing high contrast and aristocratic authority against pale backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#E0A96D` Soft Rose Gold):** Bestows metallic warmth, bespoke filigree lines, and celebratory radiance. Applied to focal emblems, badges, delicate borders, and glowing states.
- **Tertiary (`#FB7185` Pastel Rose):** Bridges the deep crimson and warm creams with gentle, vibrant affection. Reserved for floating micro-particles, active toggles, hover glows, and romantic accent hearts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFDF9` Warm Cream):** An unhurried, luminous foundation that replaces cold white with an ivory parchment feel.
- **Supporting Tones:** Tinted scrims utilize Blush Pinks (`#FFF0F5`, `#FCE7F3`) at 40–80% opacity for frosted blur surfaces, while `#BE185D` provides an intermediate energetic gradient bridge between the rose-tinted pastels and velvet wine hues.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the high-fashion, high-contrast romance of Playfair Display with the pristine, modern legibility of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- **Hero & Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys dramatic grace, literary eloquence, and bespoke intimacy. Key names and celebratory phrases can utilize italics to mimic custom calligraphic notes.
- **Body & Functional Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its gentle, humanist geometry that maintains optimal readability against frosted glass and dynamic background particles.
- **Typographic Accents:** Special memories or dedicated birthday wishes employ the `quote-accent` token in italics with generous leading, creating a lingering, heartfelt pacing.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a flexible 12-column system on desktop transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile, prioritizing generous breathing room and gallery-like pacing.

- **Desktop (>=1024px):** Max container width of 1280px with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Layout alternates between wide experiential story scrolls and staggered dual-column photo/letter arrangements.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid transitions with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column layouts compress gracefully into single focal blocks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Centered, vertical single-column journey with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem` to preserve screen real estate while protecting comfortable thumb margins for interaction.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing tokens govern breathing space between elements: `space-xl` strictly divides emotional chapters (e.g., photo timelines, message walls, gift reveals), while `space-md` and `space-lg` orchestrate inner card composition.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth does not rely on sterile gray dropshadows; rather, it uses luminous, tinted ambient light and frosted glassmorphism that mirrors candlelight shining through rose quartz.

- **Surface Layering:** 
  - Base canvas: `#FFFDF9` layered with ambient radial gradients of soft blush (`#FCE7F3` at 40% blur) and champagne (`#F5D0C5` at 25% blur).
  - Floating panels: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`.
- **Borders & Edge Highlights:** Glass containers feature low-contrast, hairline outer strokes: `1px solid rgba(245, 208, 197, 0.5)` with an inner top-edge highlight of `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` to capture simulated gold and glass reflection.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Cast shadows are multi-layered, colored, and diffused:
  - Resting Card: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(136, 19, 55, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.06)`
  - Elevated/Interactive Element: `0 20px 48px -10px rgba(136, 19, 55, 0.16), 0 0 24px 2px rgba(251, 113, 131, 0.2)`

## Shapes
The system utilizes a refined roundedness language (Level 2), where base elements inherit smooth, pebble-like curvature that feels organic and touchable.

- Standard buttons, chips, and input fields utilize `rounded` (0.5rem) to `rounded-lg` (1rem).
- Prominent memorial cards, glass message containers, and photo frames use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) to soften viewport boundaries.
- Badges, status markers, and pill buttons utilize a complete rounded contour (`rounded-full`) to complement heart emblems and delicate sparkling accents.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Button:* Rich velvet rose background (`#881337`) with warm cream typography (`#FFFDF9`), soft inner border highlight, and a rose-gold box shadow. Hover elevates the button with a glowing halo (`rgba(251, 113, 131, 0.35)`).
  - *Secondary / Glass Button:* Frosted translucent blush with a 1px soft rose-gold border (`#E0A96D`) and deep romantic rose text; hover triggers a soft champagne gradient wash.
- **Glassmorphic Memory Cards:**
  - Constructed using 65% milky translucent white with heavy backdrop blur, 1px champagne-rose borders, and rounded-xl corners. 
  - Photo containers inside cards possess a subtle paper border and gentle tilt rotation (-1deg to 1deg) to simulate physical keepsake photographs.
- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags displaying date stamps or personal traits. Rendered in pale blush (`#FCE7F3`) with delicate rose text (`#BE185D`) and micro gold star/heart icons.
- **Input Fields & Memory Wall Form:**
  - Translucent fields (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) with recessed interior shadows, gold-accented focus borders (`#E0A96D`), and romantic rose floating labels.
- **Heart & Sparkling Accents:**
  - Micro decorative components that pulse gently or track cursor movement: fine metallic rose-gold starlight stars and soft rose floating heart silhouettes with slight opacity gradients.
- **Audio & Celebration Controls:**
  - Compact glass player widgets anchored to the viewport edge, enclosed in frosted pills featuring miniature gold wave animations and gentle pulse effects.
